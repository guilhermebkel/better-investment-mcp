Status: stopped. No adapter is written. The source is `excluded` in `scripts/source-coverage.json` with this reason (issue #59, phase 2f).

## What was checked on 2026-11-15

- `https://www.gov.br/anp/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/dados-abertos` answers HTTP 200. Its licence link is **Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 3.0** (`creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nd/3.0`).
- CC BY-ND permits redistribution with attribution and forbids derivative works. The server stores sums and shares of the file, which are derived values. The licence of the open data is therefore a question the owner must decide before an adapter is written. No automated-use ban was found.
- The terms page `https://www.gov.br/anp/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/termos-de-uso-privacidade-e-seguranca-1` is the portal's general page. It holds no sentence on the reuse of the data, so no sentence is quoted.
- The dataset of fuel sales per distributor was not located on the reachable pages in this phase, so no URL and no columns are recorded.

## Next step

The owner decides whether derived values of an ND-licensed dataset are accepted. Then the dataset of sales per distributor is located, its URL and columns are recorded here, and the phase starts again.
